01 · Summary

A booking-first website for Wayne Radix

Savvy Tech will design, build and launch a mobile-first website that lets visitors book an airport transfer, reserve an island tour or rent a vehicle from Wayne Radix in a few taps. The site is built to win bookings from tourists, cruise passengers and locals, and to be found on Google when they search.

This proposal sets out what we will deliver, answers the questions raised about labour, maintenance, maps, social media and security, and confirms the full investment, with no hidden costs.

Goals for the website

  • Take taxi, airport transfer, tour and rental requests online, day or night.
  • Show the fleet, tours and rates clearly, in EC$ and US$.
  • Build trust with reviews, photos, licensing details and clear policies.
  • Rank on Google for searches such as "Grenada airport taxi" and "car rental Grenada".
  • Give every visitor a one-tap route to Wayne on WhatsApp.
  • Cut back-and-forth messages by collecting dates, pickup points and guest numbers up front.

04 · Your questions answered

Labour, maintenance and integrations

Select a question to expand the answer.

Is labour included in the price?
Yes, fully included

The EC$3,200 build fee covers all labour from start to finish: planning, design, development, content setup, integrations, testing, launch and handover training. There are no hourly charges and no surprise invoices.

Two rounds of design revisions are included. Any new work outside the agreed scope will be quoted in writing and approved by Wayne before it starts.

What are the monthly and annual maintenance costs?
EC$150 per month

The care plan is EC$150 per month, which is EC$1,800 per year. It starts on launch day and includes:

  • Website hosting and uptime monitoring
  • Security updates, SSL certificate renewals and regular backups
  • Small content updates such as rates, photos and tour details
  • A monthly report on visitors, bookings and top pages
  • Priority support by WhatsApp and email

The only other running cost is the domain name (for example, radixgrenada.com), which is registered in Wayne's name and billed yearly at cost.

Does it include Google Maps and location integration?
Yes, included
  • An embedded Google Map on the Contact page showing Wayne's base and the areas he serves.
  • One-tap "Get directions" links that open Google Maps or Apple Maps on the visitor's phone.
  • Pickup locations in the booking form, including the airport, cruise port, hotels and villas.
  • Setup of Wayne's Google Business Profile, linked to the website, so he appears on Google Maps searches.
Does it include social media integration?
Yes, included
  • Links to Wayne's Facebook, Instagram and TikTok in the header and footer.
  • Rich link previews, so when the site is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook or X it shows a photo, title and description.
  • WhatsApp chat buttons across the site, with booking details pre-filled.
  • An Instagram or Facebook feed on the homepage, if Wayne would like one.
How is the website kept secure?
Built in, and maintained monthly
  • HTTPS encryption on every page, shown by the padlock in the browser.
  • Spam protection on all booking and contact forms.
  • No card details stored on the website. If online deposits are added, payments are handled by a secure payment gateway.
  • Regular backups, security updates and monitoring through the care plan.
  • Wayne owns his domain, content and website files, with access handed over at launch.
What does SEO integration include?
Yes, included
  • Page titles and descriptions written around searches such as "Grenada airport taxi", "island tours Grenada" and "car rental Grenada".
  • Local business data (schema.org) that tells Google about Wayne's services, service area and contact details.
  • A sitemap and robots file, with the site submitted to Google Search Console.
  • Descriptive photo text, a clean heading structure and fast loading, all of which help rankings.
  • Google Business Profile setup, the single biggest factor for appearing in local searches.

These SEO foundations are already built into the demo website.
